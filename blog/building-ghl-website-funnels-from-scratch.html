From Zero to Launch: Building High-Converting GoHighLevel Websites & Funnels From Scratch

By Tehreem Umar - GHL Website & Funnel Expert  |  July 2026  |  10 min read

Building a high-converting website or sales funnel inside GoHighLevel (GHL) is a critical skill for modern marketing agencies and businesses. GHL offers a robust drag-and-drop page builder, native forms, integrated calendar bookings, and deep automation capabilities. In this guide, we will walk you through the end-to-end process of setting up, designing, and launching custom websites and funnels from scratch.

Domain Setup & SSL Configuration

The first step before writing a single line of copy is connecting your custom domain to GoHighLevel. This ensures your brand is fully white-labeled and professional.

  • For Websites (Root Domain): Point your root domain (e.g. yourdomain.com) using an A record to GHL's server IP: 34.119.162.246.
  • For Subdomains (Recommended for Funnels): Create a CNAME record (e.g. info.yourdomain.com) pointing to GHL's address: flash.funnels.msgsndr.com.
  • SSL Activation: Once DNS propagates, go to Settings → Domains in GHL, add the domain, and GHL will automatically issue a free Let's Encrypt SSL certificate.

GHL Website vs. Funnel: Choosing Your Structure

Understanding the core difference between the two structural elements inside the Sites tab is critical:

  • Websites (Sites → Websites): A multi-page structure with a global header/footer navigation. Perfect for authority-building, product cataloging, and generic service overview pages.
  • Funnels (Sites → Funnels): A linear, step-by-step conversion pathway without external navigation links. Ideal for paid ads campaigns, lead generation, booking appointments, or direct product sales.

💡 Pro Tip: Even if you build a complete brand website, use Funnels for your landing pages. Removing header/footer navigation links typically increases opt-in and booking rates by 25-40% because it keeps the visitor focused on a single call-to-action.

Designing for Conversions (Layouts & Styling)

GHL's builder uses a hierarchical layout system: Section → Row → Column → Element. To ensure high conversion rates and premium aesthetics, follow these rules:

  • Visual Hierarchy: Start with a clear Hero Section containing a compelling headline (H1), an explanatory sub-headline (H2), and a primary call-to-action (CTA) button above the fold.
  • Typography & Spacing: Stick to two font families (e.g. Outfit for headings, Plus Jakarta Sans for body text). Maintain generous vertical spacing (padding/margins) to let your content breathe.
  • Aesthetics: Utilize modern design patterns like glassmorphism (semi-transparent card backgrounds with subtle borders) and subtle glow gradients to establish a high-end luxury feel.

Integrating Forms & Surveys

To capture lead data, you must integrate native GHL Forms or Surveys:

F

GHL Forms

Best for simple registrations (e.g., Name, Email, Phone, Company). Keep friction low - every extra field you add reduces conversion by approximately 10%. For top-of-funnel opt-ins, use 2-3 fields maximum.

S

GHL Surveys

Perfect for pre-qualifying leads. Use multi-step slides with conditional logic. For example, if a lead reports a low budget, route them directly to a resources page instead of your booking calendar. This protects your time and ensures only qualified leads reach your calendar.

Connecting Funnel Automations & Workflows

A funnel is useless without backend automation. Once a visitor submits a form or books an appointment, a GoHighLevel workflow must handle the transition immediately:

⚡ Step-by-Step Funnel Automation Workflow
  • Trigger: Form Submitted (Filters: Form is "Contact Form")
  • Action 1: Create / Update Opportunity (Move to Stage: "New Lead")
  • Action 2: Add Tag: "funnel-lead"
  • Action 3: Send Welcome Email with requested resource / confirmation
  • Action 4: Wait 2 minutes, then send a soft value-first SMS nudge

Launch & Testing Checklist

Do not launch your funnel without performing a full end-to-end check. Follow this checklist:

1

Verify Domain & SSL

Confirm that the Custom Domain SSL is active and the URL resolves over HTTPS. Test in an incognito window to rule out browser cache issues.

2

Submit a Test Contact

Use dummy details to confirm the form works and data maps correctly into GHL Contacts. Check the phone field - it must be in E.164 format for SMS to work.

3

Verify Pipeline & Workflow

Check the GoHighLevel Contacts database to ensure the test submission was captured, and confirm that the Pipeline Opportunity was created and shifted into the correct stage.

4

Test Mobile Responsiveness

View the page on a real mobile device and adjust row/element padding where necessary. Most GHL traffic comes from mobile - a poor mobile experience will destroy your conversion rate.

5

Verify Confirmation & Thank-You Flows

Make sure form submissions redirect properly to your thank-you page, and that confirmation emails and SMS messages are sent within 60 seconds of submission.

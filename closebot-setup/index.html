05 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Closebot?

Closebot is an AI chatbot platform designed for sales and lead qualification. Instead of generic replies, it follows a conversation built around your offer: it answers questions, asks qualifying questions and books appointments.

Do I need GoHighLevel to use Closebot?

Closebot works best paired with GoHighLevel, and that is where I set it up most often. If you use a different CRM, we can look at a webhook, Make.com or Zapier connection on the discovery call.

Will the chatbot sound robotic?

No. The conversation is written in your brand’s tone and tested with realistic leads before launch. A short delay before the first reply can also make the conversation feel more natural.

Can Closebot book appointments into my calendar?

Yes. Once a lead is qualified, the bot can hand them a booking link or book them straight into your calendar, and the booking shows up in your GoHighLevel pipeline.

How long does a Closebot setup take?

It depends on scope: one website chat flow is quicker than a multi-channel system with SMS, social and several offers. You get a clear timeline after the free discovery call.

Do you offer ongoing optimization?

Yes. After launch I can review real conversations, tighten the qualification questions and update the knowledge base as your offer changes.

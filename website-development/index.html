05 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

What kinds of websites do you build?

Websites for personal brands, consultants, small businesses and online stores, from a focused one-page site to a multi-page site with a blog.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first and checked on phone, tablet and desktop screen sizes.

Do you handle SEO?

Every site includes on-page SEO basics: unique page titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap, fast loading and clean headings. Ongoing SEO such as content writing can be discussed separately.

Can my website forms go into my CRM?

Yes. Forms can send leads to your email, into GoHighLevel, or through Zapier or Make.com to other tools.

Can you build my website inside GoHighLevel?

Yes. If you already use GoHighLevel, your site and funnels can be built there so pages, forms and automations live in one place.

Can you add an AI chatbot to my website?

Yes. A Closebot AI assistant can answer questions and capture leads on your website around the clock.

01 — What You Get

Everything included, done for you.

GoHighLevel (GHL) can run your whole sales process: pipelines, email and SMS, calendars and funnels. But it only works when the workflows behind it are planned and connected properly.

I design and build those workflows for you, from the first form submission to the booked call and the reminders that make sure people actually show up.

Included in the service

  • CRM pipeline & stage design
  • Lead capture forms connected to the right pipeline
  • Automated email & SMS follow-up sequences
  • Calendar & booking page configuration
  • Appointment reminders & no-show follow-ups
  • Lead tags, custom fields & contact organization
  • Webhooks and Zapier / Make.com integrations
  • Workflow documentation for your team

05 — FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need my own GoHighLevel account?

Yes. The workflows are built inside your own GoHighLevel account or sub-account, so you keep full ownership of your data and automations.

Can you fix or improve my existing GHL workflows?

Yes. Many projects start with an audit of workflows that already exist: fixing broken triggers, removing duplicates and filling gaps in the follow-up.

Do you set up SMS and email follow-ups?

Yes. I write and configure email and SMS sequences for new leads, booked calls, reminders, no-shows and re-engagement.

Can GoHighLevel connect to my other tools?

Usually, yes. GoHighLevel supports webhooks, and Zapier or Make.com can connect it to most other tools. We confirm the right approach on the discovery call.

Will I be able to manage the workflows myself?

Yes. You get documentation of what each workflow does, and the setup is organized with clear names and tags so it stays easy to manage.

Can you add an AI chatbot to my GoHighLevel setup?

Yes. GoHighLevel pairs well with a Closebot AI assistant that qualifies leads and books calls straight into your pipeline.
